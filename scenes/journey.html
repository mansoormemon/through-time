About This Journey

Created by: Mansoor Ahmed Memon

This interactive visualization explores the evolution of the global economic divide from the post-WWII era to the 2008 financial crisis. Through a combination of maps, timelines, and economic indicators, it tells the story of how countries developed, diverged, and responded to major global events over more than five decades.

How to Explore

Use the timeline at the top to move through key moments from 1952 to 2007. Each step represents a five-year period and updates the map, economic indicators, and historical context for that point in time.

The world map provides an interactive view of global economic patterns. You can:

  • Zoom in and out using scroll or touch gestures.
  • Pan across the map by dragging.
  • Hover over countries to view their names.
  • Click a country to explore its individual economic journey.

Story Panel

The story panel provides additional context for each time period, including major global events, economic changes, and historical developments.

When a country is selected, the panel updates with a comparison between that country and the global baseline. Explore its GDP per capita growth, its position relative to the world median, and how its economic path differs from global trends.

Navigation

Use the previous and next controls to move through the timeline. Follow the journey from the post-war economic landscape through decades of growth, divergence, and the global financial crisis of 2008.
